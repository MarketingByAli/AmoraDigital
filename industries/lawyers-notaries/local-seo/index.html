Invisible locally means the qualified case calls someone else

Someone searching “employment lawyer Amsterdam” or “divorce lawyer [city]” is not browsing casually. They open Maps or the first organic results that look specialised and trustworthy — then call one of the first firms that feel serious enough for a stressful matter. A thin Google Business Profile, missing practice + city pages and weak review presence send that enquiry to the competitor who already owns the local specialist signal.

Legal local search is practice-driven and urgent but high-value. It is not a contractor catchment race or an installer emergency pack. Clients shortlist by rechtsgebied and city, weigh Bar and association cues, and — for notaries — compare conveyancing and will offices on locality and clarity. Directories and comparison platforms still compete for the same attention.

Paying for directory leads can fill diaries short-term, but owned map and organic visibility compounds every time someone in your city needs employment counsel, a divorce lawyer or a notary for transfer. Without local SEO you keep renting discovery while the firm with clearer practice + city pages and a complete GBP owns the free layer.

What we do

What is included in local SEO for law firms

Every deliverable serves a stressed searcher choosing counsel or a notary in their city — GBP, practice + city pages, ethical reviews, authority — not a relabelled trades local checklist.

Practice area + city keyword targeting

We aim at phrases people actually type under pressure: “employment lawyer Amsterdam”, “divorce lawyer [city]”, “personal injury lawyer [town]” — practice-driven, urgent and high-value, not vague “best lawyer” vanity terms.

Google Business Profile for the firm

Categories, hours, office photos and call or website actions set up for a law firm or notary office — so Maps shows a serious practice pin, not a half-empty listing that loses the first stressed call.

Practice + city landing pages

Local landing pages that pair a practice area with a city — employment in Utrecht, family law in Rotterdam — so organic and map clicks land on specialisation with local proof, not a thin doorway stub.

Reviews within ethical boundaries

Client reviews in sensitive matters need care. We design request habits that respect disciplinary and privacy limits — inviting feedback where appropriate, never pressuring parties in open family or employment disputes.

Authority signals in local search

Bar membership, specialised associations, publications and clear practice descriptors reinforce that your firm is the specialist someone searched for — authority that supports Maps and organic relevance, not decorative badges alone.

Notary-local search behaviour

Conveyancing and wills searches are strongly local and price-comparing. We align GBP and local pages with “notary [city]”, deed and will intent so buyers and sellers comparing offices find clear, local information first.

Multi-office and multi-lawyer structure

Where several offices or lawyers own distinct specialisations, we structure profiles, categories and local pages so one desk does not cannibalise another — each practice area and location keeps a clear signal.

Owned discovery vs directories

Comparison sites and lawyer directories still appear in results. Local SEO builds owned visibility in Maps and organic search so qualified cases are not only rented from listing platforms that rank above an incomplete firm profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers on SEO, AI SEO, Local SEO and digital growth from Amora Digital.

How do I rank for a practice area and city?
Combine a complete Google Business Profile with dedicated practice + city landing pages — for example employment lawyer Amsterdam or divorce lawyer in your town — plus consistent NAP citations and authority signals. Thin stubs rarely work; useful local practice pages with matching GBP categories send clearer signals. We do not promise a fixed ranking.
May I ask clients for reviews as a lawyer?
Often yes, but carefully. Sensitive family, employment or criminal matters need ethical and privacy boundaries. We design request habits that invite feedback where appropriate — after a closed matter, with informed consent — and never pressure parties mid-dispute. Your firm’s disciplinary rules always come first.
How do I build authority in Google as a law firm?
Strengthen GBP with accurate categories and office photos, publish practice + city pages, show Bar and association memberships, and grow recent reviews within ethical limits. Publications and clear specialisation descriptors support relevance for specialist searches — without guaranteeing a map-pack place.
Does this also apply to a notary office?
Yes. Notarial search around conveyancing and wills is strongly local and price-comparing. We align GBP and local pages with “notary [city]”, deed and will intent so buyers and sellers comparing offices find clear local information first.
How long does local SEO take for a law firm?
GBP and action improvements often show within a few weeks. Stable relevance for competitive practice + city terms usually needs several months of consistent work. Timelines depend on competition, directory noise and how complete your profile already is. We do not promise a fixed map position.
How is this different from a law firm website?
Local SEO is about being found in Maps and local search for practice + city queries. The website spoke converts that traffic with practice pages, profiles and discreet intake. They support each other; this page covers findability.
How do we compete with lawyer directories and comparison sites?
Directories will keep appearing. Local SEO builds owned GBP and organic signals so qualified enquiries are not only rented from listing platforms. Strong practice + city pages and a complete firm profile reduce dependence on paid directory leads over time.
What if we have multiple offices or specialised lawyers?
We structure profiles, categories and local pages so each location and specialisation keeps a clear signal — avoiding one desk cannibalising another when employment, family and notarial paths share a brand.
