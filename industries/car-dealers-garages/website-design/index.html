The real cost

A weak site loses the occasion sale and the workshop booking

A driver shortlisting cars after evening portal browsing compares live stock photos, whether finance and trade-in are obvious, and whether the garage looks trustworthy for APK — not only which AutoScout listing loads first. A site stuck on a static PDF of last month’s lot or missing online booking sends both the vehicle enquiry and the workshop diary slot to the competitor whose feed and APK form already look operational. Mileage, year and price filters that fail on a phone are enough for that buyer to stay inside the marketplace app and never open your brand site.

Car dealers and garages do not need a webshop cart or an accountant package table. They need two engines on one site: filterable high-value stock (often synced from portals, not sold in-cart), and recurring workshop paths for APK, maintenance, tyres and airco. Miss that split, and you pay in unsold cars and empty ramps — while drivers who fear opaque garage bills book elsewhere. The occasion sale is a considered, high-ticket decision; the workshop visit is recurring trust. One generic “Contact us” form serves neither path well.

Portals own a lot of occasion traffic, but your website is where you control brand, capture trade-in and finance leads without renting every click, and prove workshop trust that a marketplace ad alone does not tell. When two yards look equal on stock apps, the one that books APK cleanly and shows reviews online often wins the next visit. Opening hours and a call button on mobile matter the same day someone needs a slot before an inspection deadline — that is garage reality, not e-commerce conversion theory.

What we build

What is included in a car dealer & garage website

Each block serves a stock hunter or a workshop booker — portal sync, trade-in, APK booking, reviews — not automotive boilerplate with your logo, and not a generic e-commerce theme.

Occasion stock with real filters

Buyers browse by make, model, price, year and mileage — detail pages with photos and key specs. High-value, low-volume inventory, not a shopping-cart webshop checkout for socks.

Portal sync where your stack allows

Feed stock from AutoScout24, Marktplaats, Gaspedaal or similar into the site so what drivers see matches your published listings — without double entry after every arrival.

Trade-in and finance request paths

Per vehicle or site-wide: request a trade-in valuation or finance conversation without hunting a salesperson’s private number — leads land with the car reference attached.

APK and maintenance booking online

Drivers book APK, service or tyre appointments from their phone — the recurring workshop engine, separate from occasion enquiry so urgent maintenance does not hide behind stock carousels.

Workshop services laid out clearly

APK, maintenance, repairs, tyres and airco each get plain-language blocks — so someone afraid of a “expensive garage” sees what you actually do before they call.

Reviews against price fear

Prominent driver reviews next to booking and stock CTAs — trust that calms the fear of opaque bills when two garages look the same on Maps.

Call button and opening hours always visible

Phone and hours stay reachable when someone needs a same-day APK slot or a quick stock question — mobile-first, not buried under a contact form labyrinth.

Mobile for stock hunters and garage seekers

Most people browse occasions or search a garage on their phone between errands. Filters, booking CTAs and call actions stay usable with one thumb.

How we work

How an automotive website project runs

From the first walkthrough of your stock feed and workshop booking flow to measuring which pages drive occasion enquiries and APK appointments.

01

Discovery on the forecourt

We map how drivers find you today: portals, Google, referrals and walk-ins. We note your stock feed stack, workshop booking habits, brand focus and where occasion or APK enquiries get lost between the lot, the phone and a half-finished contact form.

02

Stock and workshop architecture

Information architecture separates occasion search, trade-in and finance, APK booking, workshop services and trust. Stock detail and workshop booking each get a defined slot — not one mega-menu labelled “Cars & services”.

03

Design for scanability and trust

Visual design that puts cars and booking first: readable stock cards on a phone, CTAs that say “Book APK” or “Enquire about this car”, and space for reviews without clutter.

04

Build, integrate, train

We build the site, connect your stock feed where possible, set booking and enquiry forms, and train the team to manage content without a developer for every new arrival or service update. Filters, vehicle photos and workshop hours stay editable by people who sell cars and book APK — not only by IT.

05

Launch and measure both engines

Launch tracks stock enquiries, trade-in and finance starts, APK and maintenance bookings and which pages drivers read longest. You see whether portals, Google or walk-ins fill sales and workshop — not anonymous hits that never reach the desk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers on SEO, AI SEO, Local SEO and digital growth from Amora Digital.

Can my stock sync with AutoScout24 or Marktplaats?
Where your stack and portal feeds allow, we connect published listings so the website shows the same cars as AutoScout24, Marktplaats, Gaspedaal or similar — without retyping every arrival. Exact connectors depend on your DMS, feed format and portal contracts; we map that in discovery before promising a specific integration.
Can customers book an APK or maintenance appointment online?
Yes. We put APK, service, tyre and similar workshop requests on paths drivers can reach from a phone in one or two taps, separate from occasion enquiry so urgent maintenance does not hide behind stock carousels. Booking tooling can be a form-to-diary flow or a calendar integration depending on how your workshop already plans slots.
Can I offer trade-in and finance requests on the site?
Yes. Per vehicle or site-wide forms capture trade-in valuation and finance conversation requests with the car reference attached, so sales can follow up without hunting a private mobile number. This is a lead path for high-value stock — not an in-cart checkout like a webshop.
How do I show reviews so drivers trust the garage?
We place recent, specific driver reviews next to APK booking and stock CTAs so trust sits where scanners decide. That reduces the fear of opaque bills when two garages look the same on Maps. We do not invent ratings; we surface authentic proof you already have or are building.
Does the occasion module work well on mobile?
Yes. Filters for make, model, price, year and mileage, stock cards and enquire CTAs are built for one-thumb use — because most people browse occasions or search a garage between errands on their phone. If filters break on a small screen, stock hunters leave for a portal that already works.
Is this the same as building a webshop?
No. A dealer site manages high-value, low-volume inventory that is often synced from portals and sold via enquiry, viewing and finance — not a shopping cart for low-ticket SKUs. Workshop booking is a second revenue engine. Treating it like generic e-commerce usually ships the wrong checkout and the wrong trust signals.
Can I show brand specialisation and workshop services clearly?
Yes. Brand focus pages and plain-language blocks for APK, maintenance, repairs, tyres and airco help drivers see what you actually do before they call. Clear service layout is part of winning drivers who fear a “expensive garage” without knowing what is included.
How long does a car dealer or garage website project take?
Most dealer-garage sites launch in about four to eight weeks, depending on feed complexity, booking tooling, workshop service depth and content readiness. Discovery starts with how stock and APK enquiries arrive today so we do not ship a generic brochure template.
